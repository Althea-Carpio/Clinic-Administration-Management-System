ABOUT US

Clinic Administration

The Clinic Administration Management System (CAMS) is a digital platform designed to help the school clinic efficiently manage student health records and daily clinic visits. It provides authorized clinic personnel with an organized and centralized way to record, update, and access essential student health information.

OUR SERVICES

Our Services

🩺 Student Health Records

Manage and maintain students' School Health Examination Cards in one centralized system.

📋 Daily Treatment Records

Record patient time-in, relevant information, treatment provided, and time-out for every clinic visit.

📖 Treatment History

Review a student's previous clinic visits and treatment records for easier reference.

🔐 Secure Record Management

Keep student health information accessible only to authorized clinic personnel.

MISSION, VISION & COMMITMENT

Our Purpose

Mission

The School Health and Nutrition Section is committed to provide effective health and nutrition service and to utilize these service as fertile Awareness for the inculcation of positive health values and behavior pursued with the spirit of dedication, high sense of mission and excellence towards the Development of a healthier and productive school populace in a clean healthy and Drug-free school and community environment.

Vision

The coming of the New Millennium will see dynamic innovation and service-oriented school health personnel that can help provide access to health and nutrition education activities, develop health habits and attitude among school populace that will make them capable of facing the challenge of the 21st century.

Commitment

The School Health and Nutrition Section is committed to attain and maintain a healthy environment with the aim in view of having a physically and mentally fit citizenry.
